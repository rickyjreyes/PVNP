## Before changing repository visibility

1. Identify patent families and original priority dates that might overlap
   finite discrete WCC, wavefield computing, curvature/phase readout,
   resource accounting, and any newly added implementation details.
2. Compare **the exact specification and claim support**, including drawings,
   of filed applications against **every source file and historical commit**
   that would become newly public. A filed title or provisional receipt
   alone does not establish that all later-added subject matter is supported.
3. Mark content already publicly disclosed in 2025 Zenodo records separately
   from newly disclosed 2026 code, proofs, device embodiments, and commits.
4. Decide whether any new material should be filed, withheld on a private
   branch, redacted from a purpose-built clean public repository, or released.
   Removing a file from the current tree is insufficient if its historical
   commit becomes public; do not rely on a superficial removal.
5. Consider U.S. and international patentability separately; do not assume
   that a U.S. grace period protects foreign rights or added matter.
6. Record author / qualified patent reviewer sign-off and exact approved
   commit privately, without recording privileged reasoning in Git.
