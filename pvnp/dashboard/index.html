PVNP Lean Formalization Progress

Each manuscript claim is checked against a kernel-checked Lean statement. Exact proofs, restricted results, refutations, conditional implications and open obligations are counted separately.

These percentages are not progress toward proving P ≠ NP. They count manuscript statements whose exact form is kernel-checked in Lean. The central theorem, CrossingSAT.SATLowerBound (no polynomial-time machine decides dense SAT), is unproved, and no percentage is assigned to it.

All manuscript claims

Expand a claim for its Lean declarations, status details, conditional premises and original statement. Filters apply to the selected view.

Exact
The claim, with its own hypotheses and quantifiers, is a kernel-checked Lean theorem. For an implication, only the implication is verified, not its premises.
Restricted
Lean proves the statement for a narrower model or encoding than the manuscript states.
Refuted
The claim as stated is false, and a Lean counterexample satisfies its own hypotheses.
Open
A conjecture or hypothesis stated as such, or a claim that is not well posed or not yet formalized.
