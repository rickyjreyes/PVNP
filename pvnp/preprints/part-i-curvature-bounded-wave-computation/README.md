## Research objective

Investigate whether a physical WCT curvature resource budget creates an obstruction for SAT realizations not captured by discrete Turing running time.

## Corrected mathematical status

This revision corrects the Boolean clause penalty and signed-literal encoding, separates logical satisfiability from PDE stationarity, records a dimension-lock counterexample, and treats universal curvature lower bounds as **conditional**. It distinguishes a supplied-assignment verification language (in classical `P`) from the SAT decision problem.

**It does not prove** classical `P != NP`, a universal exponential classical SAT time lower bound, or an unconditional physical WCC separation. The original illustrative plot was not supplied; the source renders a clearly labeled placeholder while keeping its reported table.

The historical source is compiled temporarily and bound into the [single current PVNP volume](../consolidated-wave-computation/README.md). The older standalone PDF is an archive artifact, not a separate new primary release.
